01 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of place, mobility, and equity.

I am a GIScientist and Ph.D. researcher at the University of South Florida. My work combines GIS, spatial statistics, network analysis, and data science to understand how transportation and infrastructure are distributed across space—and who benefits from them.

My current research includes electric-vehicle charging infrastructure and adoption, transportation safety, spatial accessibility, GeoAI, and socioeconomic disparities. I previously completed an M.S. in Geography at Texas State University, where my thesis examined hospital accessibility and socioeconomic factors in Central Texas.
